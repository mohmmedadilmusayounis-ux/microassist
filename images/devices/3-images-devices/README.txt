Laboratory instrument photographs
=================================

One photograph per instrument, in this folder, with exactly the file name shown.
Each instrument page shows a neutral placeholder box until its photo is present.
The credit line on each page comes from the instrument's "photo" entry in
site/instruments.js (w, h, by, lic, src, cap) - update it whenever a photo changes.

Current photos come from Wikimedia Commons; they were resized to fit 800 x 600 px and
converted to WebP, so CC BY / CC BY-SA photos are marked "(modified)".

Recommended format for new photos
- About 800 px wide (4:3 suits the page), under 300 KB, WebP preferred.
- Plain background; no patient information or identifiable faces.

Attribution and permission
- Use only your own photos, photos with written permission, or openly licensed photos
  (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA). Record photographer, licence and source link in the photo entry.
- Avoid manufacturer marketing images unless the manufacturer has given permission.
